About Fall TV September


Objectives

This site will be used by various types of theater artist and fans of the arts. Considering this audience, it is our objective to provide a web page which is relative to my uncle’s business. The theater name is Twelve Months of September, so he requests that the site’s title is integrated into the name and the leadership team decided upon Fall – TV – September. Maintaining relativity, through the design pattern displayed I have provided my interpretation of a calm fall day in September and captured this cozy feeling with intentional choice of color pallet, content, and ease of navigation and relatable sounds. It is my hopes that the site invites users to subscribe and become an active part of FTS's online theater community.



Aesthetics

The Home page will guide the user through the site with links to essential content starting with the “Account information” inclusive of personal information, username, passwords, and login features. The next link available on the home page is to the “About” where viewers can learn more about the site and also access the Home, Account page. The Account page will have a sign in which provides user access to the Gallery and Lobby Area(Blog) where inventory contents are accessible to share,observe, or purchase through direct payment services. The Home page will also provide a link to the “Blog” where non-members can read only; while members will have creator access to post and respond in reference to artistic views within the lobby area. The page will include information that simplifies navigation like how to register for membership, forgot user id or password, and graphics to advertise blog interaction as well as featured media items. Use of industry standards of topogrophy are implemented to ease navigations.



Future Outlook

Moving forward it is my plan to create a site that will provide an online link to the theater. One that allows individuals from all walks of life access with free subscription to our online theater. Fall TV September will offer a platform where users/viewers can gain access to exclusive showings and concession stands during events. We also will provide a page which links directly with delivery services relevant to the broadcasting event who will host concessions. This site will provide users with access to featured local merchandise, food, and delivery services. This site will provide COVID/OMICRON precautionary resources. The goal is to re-establish opportunity by safely sharing much needed arts. The pandemic recently disrupted the ways of the world as many theaters including my uncle's -Twelve Months of September was forced closed. It is my intent to provided features that will remind communities nationwide of the true and genuine value gained through continuously celebrating local artist(creators and performer),vendors, and logistics teams. This platform will help fulfill the demand for communal unification and logistics development surrounding the theater. These specific community events will be viewable online for world wide access allowing individuals to share in the festivities from other cities from afar with online ordering of nonperishable items, or when touring in a hosting city tapping into the local taste festivities offered through delivery services. To accomplish this FTS's site will be updated with a click to your city feature to accomodate these features.
